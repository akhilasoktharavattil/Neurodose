About this prototype and future drug library

No drug-specific dosing recommendations are preloaded. A production version should use a reviewed, version-controlled drug reference with citations, age/weight bands, indication-specific regimens, maximum doses, renal/hepatic rules, interaction checks, and clinical governance. Every rule should be reviewed by qualified clinicians and a pharmacist before release.

NeuroDose is an educational calculation prototype, not a medical device or prescribing authority. It stores no patient records and does not send entered values to a server. Confirm all doses independently before clinical use.
